Audit matrix — preliminary, not locked

Math
Same pairwise contrast ratio in reverse direction. No mathematical advantage from contrast magnitude alone.
Same pairwise contrast ratio in reverse direction. No mathematical advantage from contrast magnitude alone.
Hierarchy
Darker surface naturally reads as inset/sunken relative to the canvas.
Lighter surface naturally supports card/elevated hierarchy over the darker canvas.
Soft shadow
Black shadow can become weak quickly because the surface is already near-black; shadow mainly helps at boundaries where the surrounding context permits it.
Black shadow has a clearer luminance direction against the lighter surface, giving a larger usable parameter range for very soft elevation.
Soft border
Usually requires a lighter border to be seen against the darker surface; too much strength risks a visible ring.
Can use a darker border against the lighter surface; this can preserve a quieter edge while retaining separation.
Nesting
Best suited to recessed/nested regions, but upward card hierarchy has less room before approaching the canvas.
Gives a more direct path for canvas → surface → elevated → nested variations, subject to a tested surface ladder.
Current hypothesis: B is structurally promising for a general-purpose dark UI elevation system, while A may be valuable as the dedicated sunken/inset branch. This is a hypothesis, not a locked decision. Next evidence gate: surface ladder + border polarity + shadow softness + 3-level nesting under identical geometry.
