UI99 / DESIGN LAB / SPRINT 02

Nested Composition Decision Engine

A composition experiment: every component is evaluated against its actual parent surface, nesting depth, role, size and elevation. The engine decides whether fill separation alone is enough, whether edge reinforcement is needed, and whether depth should be communicated. The goal is not to decorate cards; it is to derive a coherent visual hierarchy across a real component tree.

EXPERIMENTAL · NOT LOCKED
01

Composition inputs

The important input is no longer an isolated pair. Each node is evaluated against its real parent and its position inside the hierarchy.

Decision engine

Border and shadow are separate channels. Border answers “is the edge clear?” Shadow answers “is there depth?”. The engine avoids using both unless the current separation needs both signals.
