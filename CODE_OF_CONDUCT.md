# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want LIMINAL to be a place where anyone can contribute — regardless of
experience level, language, nationality, gender identity, disability, appearance,
or religion. We pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints, and of the fact that contributors work in
  different languages and time zones.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith. Ask before concluding that someone acted in bad faith.
- Respect that maintainers' time is finite, and that "no" is a legitimate answer.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or bad-faith arguing.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, code
review — and when an individual is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer via a
[GitHub Security Advisory](https://github.com/starliTrade/UI99-Design-Lab/security/advisories/new)
(reports are private) or a direct GitHub message. All reports are reviewed and
handled confidentially. Maintainers who do not follow this code may face
temporary or permanent consequences as determined by the project's maintainer.

## Enforcement

Responses escalate with severity and repetition: a private warning, a temporary
ban from interaction, then a permanent ban. The maintainer will prioritise the
safety of the reporter.
